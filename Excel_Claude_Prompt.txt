KEY FORMULAS TO VERIFY (Excel syntax)
================================================================================

Effective Flex %:
=SUMPRODUCT(WorkloadMix_Range, CurtailmentFloor_Range)

Curtailable MW:
=SiteMW * EffectiveFlexPct

Queue Acceleration (Texas):
=ROUND(MAX(0,(TXQueueNoFlex−TXQueueFlex)*MIN(1,EffectiveFlexPct/15%)),1)

Time-to-Power (Texas):
=TXAnnualGPURevenue * (TXAccelMo/12)

Annual Spike Savings (Texas):
=TXCurtailMW * TXSpikeHours * (TXSpikePrice−TXBasePower) / 1000000

Annual DR Cap Revenue (Texas):
=TXCurtailMW * TXDRCapRate * 1000 / 1000000

PJM UCAP (Virginia):
=VACurtailMW * VAAccreditation * MIN(1, 0.5 + EffectiveFlexPct/20%)

Annual PJM Cap Revenue (Virginia):
=VADUCAP * VACap_Price_per_MW_day * 365 / 1000000

NPV calculation:
=InitialBenefit + NPV(WACC, AnnualNetBenefits_Year1_to_10)
[Note: Excel NPV() function assumes end-of-period cash flows starting Year 1]

Portfolio NPV:
=TexasNPV + ArizonaNPV + VirginiaNPV

Revenue Delta %:
=(FlexRevenue − BaselineRevenue) / BaselineRevenue
